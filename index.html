Comfort isn't a compromise on style

Most brands make you pick a lane — dress shoes that punish your feet, or sneakers that don't belong past the gym. We build every last around a memory-foam midsole first, then finish it in whatever the collection calls for: calfskin, canvas, or knit.

Each pair is fitted by hand in small batches, tested on actual pavement before it ships, and backed by a break-in guarantee — if it isn't comfortable in 48 hours, we resole it or take it back.
